HOW TO PLAY

MOVE
Tap anywhere to hop exactly one orbit at a time. Move outward one ring at a time, then back inward one ring at a time.
GATES
Each glowing gate blocks one orbit. Stay on a safe orbit or hop before the gate reaches you.
POWERS
Shield = blocks one hit. Phase = pass through obstacles for 5 seconds. Extra Life = restores one life, only when you have fewer than 3.
LIVES
You start with 3 lives. A hit removes one life. Recover with the Extra Life power.
ADVENTURE
New orbits unlock later in the run. Every unlock makes the game faster and harder. When a new orbit arrives, ⚠ appears and the countdown runs before the action resumes.
GOAL
Survive, chain clean hops, use powers when needed and beat your best score.
